Brand History

1995

Founding of BYD

Founded in Shenzhen as a rechargeable battery manufacturer specializing in nickel-cadmium and lithium-ion batteries.

2003

Automotive Entry

Acquired Tsinchuan Auto to form BYD Auto, combining battery manufacturing with vehicle engineering.

2008

World's First Plug-In Hybrid

Launched the F3DM, the world's first mass-produced dual-mode plug-in hybrid electric vehicle.

2020

Blade Battery Debut

Unveiled the Blade Battery, setting new industry standards for EV battery safety and density.
